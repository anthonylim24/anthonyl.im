Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

anthonyl.im is Anthony Lim's personal site. It hosts three apps: Lim, a chatbot that answers questions about Anthony (anthonyl.im); BreathFlow, a guided breathing app (/breathwork); and Trips, a trip planner you sign in to (/trips). This policy covers all three.

Signing in

Trips, and cloud sync in BreathFlow, need an account. Sign-in is handled by Clerk. You can sign in with an email code, a password, or your Google account.

If you choose Sign in with Google, the site receives only your name, email address, and profile picture (the openid, email, and profile scopes). It uses them to create your account, show who you are, and show your name to people you share a trip with. It does not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts, or any other Google data.

anthonyl.im's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.

What is stored

  • Account: your name, email address, and profile picture, kept by Clerk.
  • Trips: the trips you create or join (destinations, dates, places, notes, bookings), the people you share them with, and invite links. Stored in a database hosted by Supabase.
  • Places from Instagram: post links you paste in and the places found in them, kept for your account.
  • BreathFlow: settings, session history, and progress. Kept in your browser, and synced to Supabase if you sign in.
  • Lim chat history: kept only in your browser's local storage.

Services that process your data

  • AI models. Messages you send to Lim go to a hosted model through Kluster. Trip drafts, suggestions, and the trip concierge send your trip's contents to Google Gemini, with Groq and Cerebras as fallbacks. Text from Instagram posts you import is read by the same models, and audio is transcribed with Groq. Only the content needed for the request is sent.
  • Instagram posts. When you import a post, its public caption and media are fetched through Bright Data.
  • Maps and places. Searches and map views use OpenFreeMap, Photon (OpenStreetMap), Open-Meteo, Google Maps, and Kakao. The 3D view loads Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles.
  • Analytics. Page views and app usage are measured with PostHog and Inflect. These do not receive your trips or chat messages.
  • Hosting. The site runs on DigitalOcean behind Cloudflare, which process standard request data such as IP address and browser type.

What is never done

  • Your data is never sold or rented.
  • Your data is never used for advertising.
  • Your data is never shared with anyone except the services above, and only to run the apps.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete any trip you own from inside Trips, remove saved Instagram places, and clear BreathFlow and Lim data by clearing this site's storage in your browser. To delete your account and everything tied to it, ask using the contact below. It is removed within 30 days.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub, or use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
