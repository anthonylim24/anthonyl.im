Grok Bot

SMS Consent & Messaging Terms

This page documents how consumers consent to receive SMS messages from Grok Bot, what those messages contain, and how to opt out. It is provided as proof of consent for Twilio messaging verification.

Program overview

Grok Bot sends account notification alerts and status updates related to a user’s Grok Bot account. Message content may include service alerts, account updates, and other important notifications.

Message frequency: up to 1 message per day.
Rates: Message and data rates may apply.
Carrier liability: Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How opt-in (consent) is collected

Consent is collected via text. A consumer opts in by sending a keyword to the Grok Bot toll-free messaging number associated with this program.

Opt-in action: Text JOIN or START to the Grok Bot toll-free number.

Only consumers who send an affirmative opt-in keyword are enrolled. No one is enrolled automatically from browsing this website or from purchasing a product.

Opt-in confirmation message

After a successful opt-in, Grok Bot sends a confirmation similar to:

Grok Bot: You're subscribed to daily account alerts and updates. Msg frequency: up to 1/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

Sample outbound message

Grok Bot: Account alert — your status update is ready. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Msg & data rates may apply.

HELP and STOP

  • Reply STOP at any time to cancel. You will receive a one-time confirmation that you have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages unless you opt in again.
  • Reply HELP for help. You will receive contact information for support.

Sample HELP reply:

Grok Bot: For support, visit https://anthonyl.im/sms-consent or email the address listed on that page. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Msg & data rates may apply.

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers collected for this SMS program are used only to deliver the messages described above and to process HELP / STOP requests. We do not sell or share mobile opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

For questions about this messaging program, contact: hello@anthonyl.im.
